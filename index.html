Compliance

Where GDPR ends and Duty of Care begins

Two separate obligations sit on the same skip. Data protection law makes you responsible for personal data until it is irrecoverable. Waste legislation makes you responsible for where the material physically goes. A compliant confidential waste service has to answer both, in writing.

Your data protection obligation

Personal data must be disposed of securely and demonstrably. The certificate of destruction is the evidence that the data no longer exists.

Your Duty of Care obligation

Waste must pass only to an authorised carrier, with a Waste Transfer Note for every movement. Ours is issued and countersigned at collection.

What accreditation actually proves

EN 15713 governs how the destruction is carried out. ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 govern how consistently, and at what environmental cost. We hold all three.

FAQ

Confidential waste questions

Is Pearce accredited for the destruction of confidential waste?

Yes. We destroy to EN 15713, the European standard for the secure destruction of confidential material, and operate under ISO 9001 and ISO 14001. Certificates are supplied with your quote so procurement can file them up front.

How long before my material is destroyed after collection?

Typically within 24 hours of arriving at our facility. Material is held in a CCTV-monitored secure area until it is shredded, never in an open yard.

Are any of your confidential services outsourced?

No. Containment, collection, shredding and documentation are all delivered by Pearce staff using Pearce vehicles and our own plant. Nothing in the chain is brokered to a third party.

What paperwork will I receive?

A Waste Transfer Note, issued and countersigned at the point of collection, and a certificate of destruction issued with your invoice. Together they close both your Duty of Care and data protection audit trail.

Can you handle annual archive clearances?

Yes. Retention cut-offs, office moves and records-store clearances are quoted as ad hoc collections with no ongoing contract. We deliver sacks or bins in advance and collect on an agreed date.

What happens to the material after it is destroyed?

Shredded paper is intermixed and shipped directly to a UK paper mill, where it is recycled into hygiene products. Tonnage is reported back to you for your environmental reporting.
